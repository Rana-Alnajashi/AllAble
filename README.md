# CarbMate  💙

> **Automating life-critical calculations to replace daily panic with comforting precision.**

CarbMate is an iOS application designed to automate carbohydrate and insulin dosage calculations for parents of children with Type 1 Diabetes. By replacing scattered papers, manual timers, and complex standard formulas with a guided, automated interface, CarbMate eliminates calculation errors, reduces cognitive overload, and acts as a daily safeguard for families.

## 📖 The Story Behind the App

Imagine solving a life-critical equation five times a day, knowing a single mistake could result in hospitalization. This is the hidden reality for parents managing Type 1 Diabetes. 

CarbMate emerged not from a desire to code, but from witnessing this cognitive overload firsthand. When a close friend’s family received a diagnosis, it became clear that manual logs and complex formulas created a cycle of fear rather than care. CarbMate was built to bridge the gap between clinical precision and user simplicity—engineering standard endocrinology formulas into a reliable, automated system built with deep responsibility.

### 🌟 Recognition
CarbMate was selected for presentation to the **Apple Health team**, where it was showcased to **Dr. Sumbul Desai, Apple’s VP of Health**, validating the solution's ability to address a critical clinical pain point through thoughtful software engineering.

## ✨ Key Features

* **Automated Dosage Engine:** Translates complex clinical formulas (insulin-to-carb ratios, correction factors) into a simple, reliable input system, significantly reducing daily calculation errors.
* **Pre-Bolus & Flexible Timers:** Replaces manual tracking with automated alerts for optimal insulin timing, reducing the mental burden on parents.
* **Empathy-Driven UI:** A comforting, guided interface featuring motivational feedback to replace the anxiety of standard medical trackers.
* **Offline Reliability:** Fully functional without an internet connection, ensuring life-saving calculations are always available when needed.
* **Caregiver Testing:** Validated through pilot testing with local families, who reported saved time and a measurable reduction in daily cognitive load.
